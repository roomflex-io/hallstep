Privacy policy

This site is a public brochure for The Wedding Company, a planning studio run by Muhammad. We collect as little as we can, and only to reply to you.

Who we are

The Wedding Company is a UK wedding-planning studio. For privacy questions, use the contact page and mark the message as privacy.

What this site is (and is not)

Today the site is home, contact and this policy. There are no accounts, no payments, and no booking tools. If that changes, this page will change with it.

What we collect

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive:

your name, your email address, anything you write about the wedding or the message itself, and the usual technical details your email provider already attaches (time sent, and similar).

We use that only to understand what you need and to write back. We do not sell it. We do not pass it to advertisers.

What we do not collect yet

We do not ask for card details. We do not run a member login. We do not scrape your WhatsApp or your notepad — you only send what you choose to type.

Cookies and the web app

The site may store a small file so it can load faster if you add it to your home screen. That is for the pages themselves, not for tracking you across the internet. We do not run advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will say so here first.

How long we keep messages

Contact messages stay only as long as needed to answer you and follow up. They are then removed from the active inbox in the ordinary course of work.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you. You can ask us to delete a message you sent. Write via the contact page and say what you want done.

Children

This site is for adults planning a wedding. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

When the product grows, this notice will grow. The version that matters is the one on this page when you read it.
