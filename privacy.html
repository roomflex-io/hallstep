Privacy policy

This policy explains how The Wedding Company (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and looks after personal information when you use this website or contact us.

Who we are

The Wedding Company is a wedding planning studio. For privacy requests, use the contact page and mark the message as a privacy request.

Information we collect

When you contact us we collect the name, email address and message you provide, and any other details you choose to include (for example a wedding date or location).

When you visit the site, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and the date and time of the visit. This is used to operate and secure the website.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

respond to enquiries; provide planning services you ask for; improve the website; and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising networks.

Legal basis

Where UK data protection law applies, we process information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before a contract, to perform a contract, to pursue our legitimate interests in running and protecting the studio, or because you have consented (for example by sending a message).

Sharing

We share information only with people and companies who need it to provide our services — for example our email and website hosts — and only as required to do that work. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up, and then for a reasonable period in case of a later question or dispute. We then delete or anonymise them.

Cookies

The site may use essential cookies or similar storage so pages load correctly and, if you add the site to your home screen, so it can open as an app. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it, and you may object to certain processing. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is the one published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy should be sent through the contact page.
