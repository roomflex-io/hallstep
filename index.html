Why this exists

Planning my wedding was all over the place.

I’m Muhammad. When I planned my wedding, nothing lived in one place.

Ideas were in notepads. Family messages were in WhatsApp chats that never ended. Prices, halls, dresses, dates — all sitting on a long list of websites I had to keep reopening, just to remember where I’d left off.

Someone would ask a simple question and I’d be scrolling, flipping pages, opening another tab. It didn’t feel like planning a wedding. It felt like chasing the plan around the house.

I built this so other people wouldn’t have to do that. One home for the path. Not a notepad in the kitchen, a chat on the phone, and twenty sites in a browser.

This is not only a planner. A planner is a list. What you need is a way through — from “we’re doing this” to standing there together. A path to unity.

What that means in practice

Calm order. Everything that matters, together.

All in one place

The day, the people, the decisions. Not scattered across notepads, WhatsApp and a trail of websites.

A path, not a pile

We don’t dump a hundred tasks on you. We give you the next step, then the one after.

Built for the joining

Two lives, two families, sometimes two traditions. The plan should hold that — not fight it.
